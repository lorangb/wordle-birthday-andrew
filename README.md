# 🎂 Happy Birthday Andrew! — 20 Wordle Puzzles

A birthday-themed Wordle collection for Andrew James Benson — born September 6, 1983.

20 puzzles themed around 1983 pop culture, filmmaking, and Andrew's life as a producer and director.

## 🎮 How to Play

1. Guess the 5-letter word in 6 tries
2. 🟩 Green = correct letter, right spot
3. 🟨 Yellow = correct letter, wrong spot
4. ⬜ Gray = letter not in the word
5. Complete all 20 puzzles to unlock the celebration screen!

## 🧩 The Words

Each puzzle relates to:
- **1983** — Flashdance, Apple Mac launch, 80s culture
- **Filmmaking** — Andrew's world (producer, director, Emmy winner)
- **Birthday** — Party, happy, celebration vibes
- **Phoenix** — Where Andrew is from
